## Introduction

Not every false story begins with a fake image. Sometimes the image is entirely genuine and the deception lies in the caption. One of the clearest recent examples connected to [Lesotho]({{ 'how-false-stories-about-lesotho-took/' | relative_url }}) involved a viral video that appeared to show hundreds of people desperately trying to cross into South Africa. The footage was real, the crowd was real, and the location was real. What was false was the explanation attached to it. Investigators later established that the scene showed young Basotho attending a Lesotho Defence Force (LDF) recruitment exercise, not a mass flight from the country. The episode illustrates a recurring pattern in modern misinformation: authentic footage can become misleading when stripped of its original context and paired with a more dramatic narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lesotho.misa.org/2025/06/23/fact-check-viral-video-of-lesotho-citizens-fleeing-to-south-africa-is-actually-ldf-recruitment-footage/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lesotho.misa.org">[MISA Lesotho]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lesotho.misa.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lesotho Fact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MISA LesothoFact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South...June 23, 2025 — 23 Jun 2025 — The footage actually depicts B...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 23, 2025</span></span></span>

## The Crowd Wrongly Described as Fleeing Lesotho

In June 2025, a widely shared social-media post claimed that video footage showed “Lesotho citizens trying to cross borders to South Africa”. The clip depicted a tightly packed crowd, creating an impression of urgency and disorder. To many viewers, the scene appeared to confirm fears about unemployment, migration pressures and economic hardship.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lesotho.misa.org/2025/06/23/fact-check-viral-video-of-lesotho-citizens-fleeing-to-south-africa-is-actually-ldf-recruitment-footage/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lesotho.misa.org">[MISA Lesotho]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lesotho.misa.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lesotho Fact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MISA LesothoFact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South...June 23, 2025 — 23 Jun 2025 — The footage actually depicts B...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 23, 2025</span></span></span>

The claim spread easily because the visual evidence seemed convincing. Large crowds are often associated with refugee movements, border crises or political unrest. Viewers encountering only a short clip had little information about where it had been filmed, who the people were, or why they had gathered. The caption supplied a dramatic explanation, and many people accepted it without further verification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lesotho.misa.org/2025/06/23/fact-check-viral-video-of-lesotho-citizens-fleeing-to-south-africa-is-actually-ldf-recruitment-footage/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lesotho.misa.org">[MISA Lesotho]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lesotho.misa.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lesotho Fact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MISA LesothoFact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South...June 23, 2025 — 23 Jun 2025 — The footage actually depicts B...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 23, 2025</span></span></span>

This was not a fabricated video or an artificial-intelligence creation. The footage genuinely existed. The error arose because the audience was encouraged to interpret the scene through a misleading narrative rather than through its actual circumstances.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lesotho.misa.org/2025/06/23/fact-check-viral-video-of-lesotho-citizens-fleeing-to-south-africa-is-actually-ldf-recruitment-footage/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lesotho.misa.org">[MISA Lesotho]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lesotho.misa.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lesotho Fact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MISA LesothoFact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South...June 23, 2025 — 23 Jun 2025 — The footage actually depicts B...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 23, 2025</span></span></span>

## How Investigators Identified the Recruitment Event

Fact-checkers approached the claim by asking a simple question: where did the footage originally come from?

Their investigation began with reverse-image searches and frame-by-frame examination of the video. This led researchers to social-media posts showing the same location and eventually to material published by the Lesotho Defence Force itself. The original footage was traced to recruitment interviews held at Makoanyane Barracks in Maseru on 31 May 2025. A comparison between the viral clip and official LDF material showed that they were the same event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lesotho.misa.org/2025/06/23/fact-check-viral-video-of-lesotho-citizens-fleeing-to-south-africa-is-actually-ldf-recruitment-footage/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lesotho.misa.org">[MISA Lesotho]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lesotho.misa.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lesotho Fact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MISA LesothoFact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South...June 23, 2025 — 23 Jun 2025 — The footage actually depicts B...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 23, 2025</span></span></span>

## Why Authentic Images Can Still Mislead

The case demonstrates an important distinction in the study of misinformation. Falsehood does not always require manipulated media. A genuine photograph or video can become deceptive when accompanied by incorrect information about time, place or purpose.

In this instance, several factors made the false interpretation persuasive:

### The Scene Fit Existing Assumptions

Lesotho faces significant youth unemployment, making a story about citizens leaving in search of opportunities seem plausible to many viewers. Because the claim aligned with concerns people already recognised, fewer questioned the caption.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lesotho.misa.org/2025/06/23/fact-check-viral-video-of-lesotho-citizens-fleeing-to-south-africa-is-actually-ldf-recruitment-footage/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lesotho.misa.org">[MISA Lesotho]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lesotho.misa.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lesotho Fact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MISA LesothoFact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South...June 23, 2025 — 23 Jun 2025 — The footage actually depicts B...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 23, 2025</span></span></span>

### Short Videos Remove Context

Social-media clips often circulate detached from their original source. A viewer may see only a few seconds of footage, without location markers, dates or accompanying explanation. In such circumstances, the caption can become more influential than the image itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lesotho.misa.org/2025/06/23/fact-check-viral-video-of-lesotho-citizens-fleeing-to-south-africa-is-actually-ldf-recruitment-footage/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lesotho.misa.org">[MISA Lesotho]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lesotho.misa.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lesotho Fact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MISA LesothoFact Check: Viral Video of &#x27;Lesotho Citizens Fleeing to South...June 23, 2025 — 23 Jun 2025 — The footage actually depicts B...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 23, 2025</span></span></span>

### Crowds Are Visually Ambiguous

A crowd waiting for a concert, a political rally, a recruitment drive or a border crossing may look remarkably similar in a short video.
