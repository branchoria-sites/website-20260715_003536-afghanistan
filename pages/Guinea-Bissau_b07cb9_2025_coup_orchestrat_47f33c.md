## Introduction

The November 2025 military takeover in [Guinea-Bissau]({{ 'which-guinea-bissau-stories-survived-the/' | relative_url }}) is unusual within the country's long history of coups because the central dispute is not whether a takeover occurred, but whether it was what it appeared to be. Soldiers undeniably seized power, halted the electoral process and announced military control. What remains contested is whether the intervention was a genuine coup against President Umaro Sissoco Embaló or a manoeuvre designed by elements close to him to prevent an unfavourable election result from becoming official.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/sustained-gunfire-near-guinea-bissau-election-commission-building-witnesses-say-2025-11-26/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[Reuters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Army officers say they have seized power in Guinea-Bissau</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army officers say they have seized power in Guinea-BissauNovember 26, 2025 — 26 Nov 2025 — A group of army officers said they had...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 26, 2025</span></span></span>

That distinction matters because this episode sits on the boundary between a documented political event and an unresolved allegation of deception. Unlike a classic hoax that is later exposed by definitive evidence, the 2025 crisis destroyed or disrupted many of the institutions that might have settled the question. ## What happened before the results were announced?

The crisis emerged immediately after a closely fought presidential election held on 23 November 2025. Even before official results were released, both the incumbent president, Umaro Sissoco Embaló, and opposition-backed challenger Fernando Dias publicly claimed victory. Observers had expected a tight contest, making the competing declarations particularly significant.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/africa/guinea-bissau-holds-close-vote-embalo-seeks-rare-second-term-2025-11-23/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[Reuters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Guinea-Bissau votes for president with Embalo in tight race</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Guinea-Bissau votes for president with Embalo in tight race...November 23, 2025 — 23 Nov 2025 — BISSAU, Nov 23 (Reuters) - Coup-p...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 23, 2025</span></span></span>

The scheduled announcement of provisional results never arrived. On 26 November, gunfire was reported near key state institutions, including areas connected to the presidency and the electoral authorities. Shortly afterwards, military officers announced that they had taken power and suspended the electoral process. Embaló himself stated that he had been deposed and arrested, while soldiers declared that they were assuming control of the country.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/sustained-gunfire-near-guinea-bissau-election-commission-building-witnesses-say-2025-11-26/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[Reuters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Army officers say they have seized power in Guinea-Bissau</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army officers say they have seized power in Guinea-BissauNovember 26, 2025 — 26 Nov 2025 — A group of army officers said they had...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 26, 2025</span></span></span>

Regional and international observers reacted as though a coup had occurred. The African Union, ECOWAS and other international actors condemned the military intervention and called for constitutional order to be restored. Election observers also urged that electoral officials be allowed to complete their work.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.peaceau.org/en/article/press-release-african-union-commission-chairperson-unequivocally-condemns-the-military-coup-d-etat-in-guinea-bissau" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: peaceau.org">[AU Peace and Security]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">peaceau.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on peaceau.org.</span></span></span>

What transformed the event from a conventional coup into a controversy about possible deception was the timing. The military moved only days after voting and one day before official results were expected. Because no certified result was ever released, the public never learned who had actually won the election.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/sustained-gunfire-near-guinea-bissau-election-commission-building-witnesses-say-2025-11-26/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[Reuters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Army officers say they have seized power in Guinea-Bissau</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army officers say they have seized power in Guinea-BissauNovember 26, 2025 — 26 Nov 2025 — A group of army officers said they had...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 26, 2025</span></span></span>

## Why opponents called the takeover simulated

Almost immediately, opposition figures argued that the coup narrative itself might be misleading.

Fernando Dias and his supporters claimed that Embaló had realised he was likely to lose the election and that the apparent coup provided a mechanism for interrupting the count before defeat could be confirmed. Former prime minister Domingos Simões Pereira likewise accused the president of attempting to manufacture or simulate a crisis that would justify extraordinary measures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Guinea-Bissau_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">2025 Guinea Bissau coup d&#x27;état</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2025 Guinea Bissau coup d&#x27;état</span></span></span>

Several developments strengthened suspicions among critics:

* The intervention occurred just before the expected publication of election results.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/sustained-gunfire-near-guinea-bissau-election-commission-building-witnesses-say-2025-11-26/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[Reuters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Army officers say they have seized power in Guinea-Bissau</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army officers say they have seized power in Guinea-BissauNovember 26, 2025 — 26 Nov 2025 — A group of army officers said they had...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 26, 2025</span></span></span>
* Political figures aligned with Embaló retained influence after the takeover, rather than being comprehensively removed from power.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Guinea-Bissau_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">2025 Guinea Bissau coup d&#x27;état</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2025 Guinea Bissau coup d&#x27;état</span></span></span>
* Civil-society groups and some regional political figures publicly questioned whether the military action genuinely targeted the president or instead protected his interests.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/2a1371eb1ceb25596b99c4cc4062bc95" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Embaló&#x27;s presidency has been controversial, with disputes over the expiration of his term, and ongoing political instability. The militar...</span></span></span>
* The country already had a recent history of opposition accusations that alleged coup threats were being used to justify exceptional political measures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/nov/26/guinea-bissau-officers-take-total-control-close-borders-amid-election-chaos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theguardian.com">[The Guardian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theguardian.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Guardian Guinea-Bissau military takes &#x27;total control&#x27; amid election chaos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The military has formed a high command body to govern and restore order, continuing a turbulent pattern of coups in Guinea-Bissau since i...</span></span></span>

These arguments did not prove a staged operation, but they helped spread the idea that the official appearance of events might conceal a different political reality. In Guinea-Bissau's fragmented information environment, where trust in institutions has often been weak, such claims found a receptive audience. Competing camps interpreted the same events through radically different assumptions about who stood to benefit.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/9e7cfebdbd32430092d97cc291184dbc" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">AP News Guinea-Bissau&#x27;s latest coup sparks allegations of a staged takeover</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Here&#x27;s what to knowIn Guinea-Bissau, a controversial military coup took place on November 26, 2025, following a disputed presidential ele...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 26, 2025</span></span></span>

## Why others rejected the staged-coup theory

The military leadership offered a very different explanation. Officers said they had intervened because of alleged attempts to manipulate the election and because public order was under threat. Their statements portrayed the takeover as a response to a political emergency rather than part of a hidden strategy.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/2a1371eb1ceb25596b99c4cc4062bc95" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Embaló&#x27;s presidency has been controversial, with disputes over the expiration of his term, and ongoing political instability. The militar...</span></span></span>

Supporters of the genuine-coup interpretation point to several facts.

First, armed soldiers visibly seized institutions and publicly suspended constitutional processes. Second, Embaló initially described himself as having been arrested and overthrown.
