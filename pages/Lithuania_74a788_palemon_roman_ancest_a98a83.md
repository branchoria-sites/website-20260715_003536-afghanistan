## Introduction

The Palemon legend was one of the most successful invented origin stories in Lithuanian history. Beginning in early modern chronicles, it claimed that [Lithuania]({{ 'lithuania/' | relative_url }})’s ruling elite descended from Roman nobles led by a prince named Palemon who fled the Roman world and settled on the Baltic coast. Modern historians regard the story as a political myth rather than genuine history, yet for centuries it served an important purpose. It gave the Grand Duchy of Lithuania an ancient and prestigious pedigree, strengthened the status of noble families and helped answer foreign claims that Lithuania was a culturally inferior newcomer to Christian Europe. Rather than a simple fraud, the Palemon story was an example of how historical myth-making could become a tool of politics, identity and social ambition.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ldkistorija.lt/where-we-came-from-the-myth-of-the-roman-lineage-of-lithuanians/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ldkistorija.lt">[Orbis Lituaniae]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ldkistorija.lt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Orbis Lituaniae -Where We Came From: The Myth of the Roman Lineage</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 2, 2020 — The theory that the Lithuanians originated from the Romans was written down for the first time in the Lithuanian Chroni...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 2, 2020</span></span></span>

## Why Did Lithuanian Nobles Claim Roman Ancestors?

The legend emerged during a period when ancestry was a political resource. In the sixteenth century, the Grand Duchy of Lithuania was one of Europe's largest states, but its elite faced uncomfortable comparisons with older and supposedly more “civilised” powers. Lithuania had been the last pagan state in Europe to accept Christianity, and hostile writers often portrayed it as backward or dependent on Poland.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Chronicles" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lithuanian Chronicles</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lithuanian Chronicles</span></span></span>

A Roman ancestry offered a powerful answer. If Lithuanian rulers and nobles could trace their origins to ancient Rome, they possessed a lineage as distinguished as any dynasty in Europe. The myth transformed Lithuania from a peripheral land into a successor of classical civilisation. Historians have noted that the story reflected growing confidence among Lithuanian elites and their desire to present the Grand Duchy as an independent political community rather than merely a junior partner in its relationship with Poland.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ldkistorija.lt/where-we-came-from-the-myth-of-the-roman-lineage-of-lithuanians/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ldkistorija.lt">[ldkistorija.lt]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ldkistorija.lt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Orbis Lituaniae -Where We Came From: The Myth of the Roman Lineage</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 2, 2020 — The theory that the Lithuanians originated from the Romans was written down for the first time in the Lithuanian Chroni...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 2, 2020</span></span></span>

The attraction of Roman descent was not unique to Lithuania. Across Renaissance Europe, scholars and rulers searched for connections to Troy, Rome or other prestigious ancient cultures. The Palemon legend fitted this wider intellectual fashion while serving specifically Lithuanian political needs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ojs.unito.it/index.php/COSMO/article/view/6907/6056" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ojs.unito.it">[OJS Unito]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ojs.unito.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">joanna orzeł - greek and roman motifs in the mythology of...by J Orzeł · 2022 · Cited by 1 — ABSTRACT: This article analyses th...</span></span></span>

## What the Palemon Legend Claimed

The most influential version of the story appeared in the Lithuanian Chronicles during the early sixteenth century. According to the narrative, a Roman noble named Palemon fled the empire together with hundreds of aristocratic families. Different versions placed the migration under Emperor Nero in the first century or during the chaos associated with Attila the Hun centuries later. After a long sea voyage, the migrants supposedly reached the Baltic coast and travelled inland, where they founded a ruling dynasty that eventually produced Lithuania’s princes and noble houses.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ldkistorija.lt/where-we-came-from-the-myth-of-the-roman-lineage-of-lithuanians/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ldkistorija.lt">[ldkistorija.lt]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ldkistorija.lt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Orbis Lituaniae -Where We Came From: The Myth of the Roman Lineage</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 2, 2020 — The theory that the Lithuanians originated from the Romans was written down for the first time in the Lithuanian Chroni...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 2, 2020</span></span></span>

The story became increasingly elaborate as chroniclers attempted to fill gaps between Roman antiquity and medieval Lithuania. New generations of rulers were inserted into genealogies, chronologies were shifted, and additional legendary ancestors appeared. These revisions were often designed to solve obvious problems in the timeline rather than reflect any newly discovered evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palemonids" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

One revealing feature of the legend is how frequently it changed. Palemon was sometimes described as a relative of Nero, sometimes linked to other Roman noble traditions, and the date of his arrival moved repeatedly. Such flexibility suggests that chroniclers were constructing a useful narrative rather than preserving a genuine historical memory.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palemonids" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

## How Noble Families Adapted the Myth

The Palemon legend became especially valuable when noble families incorporated it into their own genealogies. If the Lithuanian state descended from Roman aristocrats, individual magnate families could claim a share of that prestige by connecting themselves to Palemon or his companions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ldkistorija.lt/genealogical-imagination/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ldkistorija.lt">[Orbis Lituaniae]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ldkistorija.lt</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Orbis Lituaniae -Genealogical ImaginationOctober 2, 2020 — The legendary forefather of the Pac was Cosimus Pacius who also together with...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 2, 2020</span></span></span>

Several influential families developed elaborate Roman ancestries:

* The Pac family promoted a connection with the famous Florentine Pazzi family, arguing that both descended from common Roman forebears associated with the Palemon migration.
* Other noble houses linked themselves to various legendary companions of Palemon and constructed family trees reaching back to antiquity.
* Genealogical traditions were expanded through chronicles, heraldic works and family histories that blended political ambition with historical imagination.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.llti.lt/failai/e-zurnalai/SLL18/XVIIIrez167-168.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: llti.lt">[llti.lt]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">llti.lt</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PACAS OR PAZZI? The Pac–Pazzi connection is particularly revealing. Similarities between family names and the prestige of Italian Renaissance aristocracy made the claim attractive, even though historians have found no evidence for an actual blood relationship. The story nevertheless circulated widely because it enhanced status and strengthened the family's image both within Lithuania and abroad.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.llti.lt/failai/e-zurnalai/SLL18/XVIIIrez167-168.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: llti.lt">[llti.lt]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">llti.lt</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PACAS OR PAZZI? In this way, the Palemon legend functioned as a social technology. It provided a ready-made framework through which elite families could present themselves as ancient, distinguished and naturally suited to leadership.

## Why People Found the Story Convincing

Modern readers may wonder why such an improbable tale gained acceptance. Part of the answer lies in the standards of historical scholarship available at the time. Early modern historians worked with limited documentary evidence and often treated legend, genealogy and history as interconnected forms of knowledge.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ldkistorija.lt/where-we-came-from-the-myth-of-the-roman-lineage-of-lithuanians/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ldkistorija.lt">[Orbis Lituaniae]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ldkistorija.lt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Orbis Lituaniae -Where We Came From: The Myth of the Roman Lineage</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 2, 2020 — The theory that the Lithuanians originated from the Romans was written down for the first time in the Lithuanian Chroni...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 2, 2020</span></span></span>

The story also contained details that made it appear credible. Chroniclers supplied names, migration routes, coats of arms and long dynastic successions. The sheer quantity of information created an impression of authenticity even when the underlying evidence was absent. Similar techniques appeared in many European foundation myths.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palemonids" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

Most importantly, the legend answered real political and cultural needs. It offered a flattering explanation of Lithuania's origins, reinforced noble identity and supplied arguments in diplomatic and ideological disputes. Stories that satisfy social expectations often survive despite weak factual foundations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ldkistorija.lt/where-we-came-from-the-myth-of-the-roman-lineage-of-lithuanians/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ldkistorija.lt">[ldkistorija.lt]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ldkistorija.lt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Orbis Lituaniae -Where We Came From: The Myth of the Roman Lineage</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 2, 2020 — The theory that the Lithuanians originated from the Romans was written down for the first time in the Lithuanian Chroni...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 2, 2020</span></span></span>

## How the Roman Origin Story Was Discredited

As historical methods became more rigorous during the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, scholars began examining the Palemon narrative critically. Researchers noticed that the chronology was inconsistent, the genealogies were repeatedly altered and no independent evidence supported the supposed migration from Rome.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palemonids" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

The absence of archaeological, linguistic and documentary evidence proved especially damaging. Nothing connected early Lithuanians to a mass settlement of Roman nobles. Instead, the legend increasingly appeared to be a literary construction developed within the political culture of the Grand Duchy itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palemonids" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

By the nineteenth century, historians such as Joachim Lelewel were openly rejecting the story as a myth. Today it is treated as an important source for understanding early modern political thought rather than a reliable account of Lithuania's origins.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palemonids" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

## A National Myth Rather Than a Historical Hoax

The Palemon legend occupies an interesting place in Lithuania's history of disputed truths. It was not a forgery in the narrow sense. No fabricated Roman document suddenly appeared to prove Palemon's existence, and the story evolved gradually through chronicles and genealogies rather than through a single act of deception.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Chronicles" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lithuanian Chronicles</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lithuanian Chronicles</span></span></span>

Yet it belongs alongside famous invented traditions because it demonstrates how a politically useful fiction can acquire authority through repetition, prestige and institutional support. For generations, the legend helped explain who Lithuanians were, where their ruling class came from and why the Grand Duchy deserved respect among European powers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ldkistorija.lt/where-we-came-from-the-myth-of-the-roman-lineage-of-lithuanians/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ldkistorija.lt">[Orbis Lituaniae]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ldkistorija.lt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Orbis Lituaniae -Where We Came From: The Myth of the Roman Lineage</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 2, 2020 — The theory that the Lithuanians originated from the Romans was written down for the first time in the Lithuanian Chroni...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 2, 2020</span></span></span>
