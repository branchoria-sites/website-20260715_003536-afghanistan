## Introduction

Were [Japan]({{ 'how-japans-most-famous-hoaxes-fell-apart/' | relative_url }})’s mermaid mummies meant to deceive? The short answer is that many almost certainly were artificial creations, but their purpose is harder to pin down than a simple fraud. Scientific examinations of several famous specimens have shown that they were assembled from paper, cloth, wood, fish parts, animal hair and other materials rather than preserved bodies of unknown creatures. Yet many of these objects spent centuries not in travelling sideshows but in temples, shrines, private collections and local traditions. Some may have been commercial curiosities. Others appear to have acquired religious or protective significance after they were made. The result is a rare category of artefact that sits between deliberate fabrication and living folklore. Modern scans can reveal how the figures were built, but they cannot always explain who built them, for whom, or what the makers expected people to believe.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.discovermagazine.com/mermaid-mummy-revealed-to-be-paper-mache-44684" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: discovermagazine.com">[discovermagazine.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">discovermagazine.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">mermaid mummy revealed to be paper mache 44684</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Discover MagazineJapanese Mermaid Mummy Revealed to Be Paper Mache9 Mar 2023 — A revered Japanese relic turned out to be a fraud, but not...</span></span></span>

## How Composite Mermaid Bodies Were Constructed

The best-known Japanese mermaid mummies resemble small creatures with human-like heads and arms attached to fish tails. For many years researchers assumed they were similar to the famous “Feejee Mermaid” type displayed by nineteenth-century showmen: stitched combinations of monkey and fish remains. Closer investigation has shown that the reality is often more complicated.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.horniman.ac.uk/story/unmasking-the-mysterious-merman/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: horniman.ac.uk">[Horniman Museum and Gardens]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">horniman.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">unmasking the mysterious merman</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Horniman Museum and GardensUnmasking the mysterious merman21 Feb 2012 — One Shinto headquarters in Fujinomiya holds a mermaid mummy reput...</span></span></span>

One of the most intensively studied examples came from Enjuin Temple in Okayama Prefecture. The figure, around 30 centimetres long, was traditionally associated with a story that it had been caught in the sea during the Edo period. When researchers subjected it to modern analysis, they discovered that its interior was largely artificial rather than biological.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://japan-forward.com/unraveling-the-mystery-of-the-sleeping-mermaid-of-okayama/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: japan-forward.com">[JAPAN Forward]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">japan-forward.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">unraveling the mystery of the sleeping mermaid of okayama</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">JAPAN ForwardUnraveling the Mystery of the Sleeping Mermaid of Okayama13 May 2022 — A local team is searching legends and science to unco...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2022</span></span></span>

The construction techniques revealed by scans suggest careful craftsmanship rather than a crude fake:

## What Scans and Material Tests Revealed

The major breakthrough in understanding Japanese mermaid mummies came from non-destructive scientific examination. Researchers used X-rays, computed tomography (CT) scanning, radiocarbon dating and material analysis to investigate specimens without dismantling them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://explorersweb.com/mermaid-mummy-japanese-temple/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: explorersweb.com">[explorersweb.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">explorersweb.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Foot-Long &#x27;Mermaid&#x27; Mummy Discovered in Japanese...12 Mar 2023 — Researchers at Kurashiki University analyzed the creature w...</span></span></span>

The Enjuin specimen produced one of the clearest results. CT scans found no hidden skeleton corresponding to a real mermaid-like animal. Instead, investigators identified a largely manufactured object. Reports described internal layers of paper, cloth and cotton, with only limited biological components incorporated into the design.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://explorersweb.com/mermaid-mummy-japanese-temple/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: explorersweb.com">[explorersweb.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">explorersweb.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Foot-Long &#x27;Mermaid&#x27; Mummy Discovered in Japanese...12 Mar 2023 — Researchers at Kurashiki University analyzed the creature w...</span></span></span>

Researchers also identified a mixture of natural and artificial elements. The hair was mammalian. Fish-derived material appeared in parts of the mouth and scales. Surface coatings included mineral and paste-like compounds used to shape and preserve the figure. Rather than discovering a preserved unknown creature, scientists uncovered evidence of skilled assembly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.newsweek.com/mermaid-monkey-mummy-japan-solved-mystery-1783044" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newsweek.com">[newsweek.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newsweek.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mystery of Creepy Mummified Monkey Mermaid Solved</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mystery of Creepy Mummified Monkey Mermaid Solved...February 22, 2023 — 22 Feb 2023 — Inside the creature&#x27;s body was only cloth...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 22, 2023</span></span></span>

The findings echoed investigations of similar “mermaid” specimens held outside Japan. Museums examining related figures have repeatedly found composite constructions made from multiple materials and species. The resemblance between many Japanese examples suggests a shared tradition of manufacturing mermaid-like curiosities rather than isolated attempts to preserve genuine creatures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.horniman.ac.uk/story/unmasking-the-mysterious-merman/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: horniman.ac.uk">[horniman.ac.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">horniman.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">unmasking the mysterious merman</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Horniman Museum and GardensUnmasking the mysterious merman21 Feb 2012 — One Shinto headquarters in Fujinomiya holds a mermaid mummy reput...</span></span></span>

Importantly, scientific testing answered only part of the mystery. It demonstrated that the bodies were manufactured, but it did not identify the individual creators or precisely reconstruct the circumstances of their production.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sciencealert.com/we-finally-know-what-japans-eerie-mummified-mer-monkey-really-is" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sciencealert.com">[ScienceAlert]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sciencealert.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">we finally know what japans eerie mummified mer monkey really is</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">We Finally Know What Japan&#x27;s Eerie Mummified Mer...21 Feb 2023 — An in-depth analysis has finally revealed the make-up of a...</span></span></span>

## Why Their Original Purpose Remains Uncertain

The strongest evidence points toward human manufacture, yet the motive remains surprisingly elusive.

A modern observer may assume that every mermaid mummy was intended to trick buyers into believing they were genuine remains. Some probably were commercial curiosities. During the Edo period and afterwards, unusual creatures attracted public interest, and exotic artefacts could command attention and money. A fabricated mermaid would have fit naturally into a market for wonders, monsters and rare specimens.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.jpost.com/archaeology/article-732116" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: jpost.com">[Jerusalem Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">jpost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">article 732116</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">article 732116</span></span></span>

However, the surviving evidence suggests a more complicated story. Many specimens ended up in temples and shrines where they became objects of reverence or local tradition. Some were associated with beliefs about protection, healing or longevity. In these settings, the object’s symbolic power may have become more important than any claim that it represented a real biological species.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://japan-forward.com/unraveling-the-mystery-of-the-sleeping-mermaid-of-okayama/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: japan-forward.com">[japan-forward.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">japan-forward.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">unraveling the mystery of the sleeping mermaid of okayama</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">JAPAN ForwardUnraveling the Mystery of the Sleeping Mermaid of Okayama13 May 2022 — A local team is searching legends and science to unco...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2022</span></span></span>

At each stage, new meanings could be attached to the same physical object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://japan-forward.com/unraveling-the-mystery-of-the-sleeping-mermaid-of-okayama/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: japan-forward.com">[japan-forward.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">japan-forward.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">unraveling the mystery of the sleeping mermaid of okayama</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">JAPAN ForwardUnraveling the Mystery of the Sleeping Mermaid of Okayama13 May 2022 — A local team is searching legends and science to unco...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2022</span></span></span>

This is why historians are often cautious about describing every mermaid mummy as a straightforward hoax. The physical object may be fabricated, but the cultural significance attached to it may have been entirely sincere.

## Between Fraud and Folklore

Japanese mermaid mummies occupy an unusual position in the history of deception. Unlike forged archaeological artefacts designed to rewrite history, these figures rarely altered scientific knowledge or public policy. Their significance lies in the blurred boundary between fabrication and belief.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.jpost.com/archaeology/article-732116" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: jpost.com">[Jerusalem Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">jpost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">article 732116</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">article 732116</span></span></span>

Folklore about mermaid-like beings existed in Japan long before modern scientific investigation. Stories linked such creatures to prophecy, misfortune, longevity and the sea. Against that background, a crafted mermaid body could serve as a tangible connection to legendary traditions. The object did not need to withstand scientific scrutiny to possess cultural value.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nippon.com/en/japan-topics/g02101/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nippon.com">[Nippon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nippon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">“Ningyo”: Japanese Merfolk and Auspicious Mummies</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">“Ningyo”: Japanese Merfolk and Auspicious Mummies</span></span></span>
