## Introduction

The Richard Lugar Center for Public Health Research in Tbilisi has become one of the most persistent targets of modern disinformation in [Georgia]({{ 'how-georgias-most-convincing-falsehoods/' | relative_url }}). Since the mid-2010s, a succession of allegations has portrayed the laboratory as a secret American biological-weapons facility, a site of human experimentation, a source of disease outbreaks, and even a centre for creating insects intended to spread pathogens. Although the specific accusations have changed repeatedly, independent inspections, public documentation, international scientific cooperation and the laboratory’s visible role in disease surveillance have consistently contradicted the claims. The story is significant not because a hidden weapons programme was uncovered, but because it shows how a conspiracy narrative can survive even when its details are repeatedly disproved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gpwmdcounterdisinfo.com/policy-briefs/the-lugar-centre-disinformation-campaign/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gpwmdcounterdisinfo.com">[gpwmdcounterdisinfo.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gpwmdcounterdisinfo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Countering WMD DisinformationThe Lugar Centre disinformation campaignThe first highly visible, contemporary and multichannel disinformati...</span></span></span>

In the wider history of Georgian hoaxes, myths and information manipulation, the Lugar case stands out as an example of organised disinformation rather than folklore, misunderstanding or commercial fraud. It demonstrates how scientific institutions can become symbols in geopolitical disputes and how public anxieties about disease can be turned into persuasive narratives.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://euvsdisinfo.eu/georgia/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: euvsdisinfo.eu">[euvsdisinfo.eu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">euvsdisinfo.eu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Georgia ArchivesDiscover EUvsDisinfo&#x27;s coverage on Georgia, exposing Russian disinformation campaigns targeting its independen...</span></span></span>

## What the laboratory actually does

The Lugar Center is part of Georgia’s public-health infrastructure. Established with American assistance through programmes intended to improve biosafety and disease monitoring, it functions as a high-level diagnostic and research facility. Its work includes identifying dangerous pathogens, supporting outbreak investigations, training specialists and maintaining secure storage of hazardous biological samples. The laboratory has also played a major role in tracking infectious diseases affecting both humans and animals.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.voanews.com/a/is-a-secret-us-lab-in-georgia-spreading-deadly-pathogens/6741681.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: voanews.com">[voanews.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">voanews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Voice of AmericaIs a Secret US Lab in Georgia Spreading Deadly...31 Oct 2017 — The Lugar Laboratory does not develop biological weapons...</span></span></span>

Because laboratories of this type handle dangerous organisms, they can appear mysterious to outsiders. Yet the activities that conspiracy theories often present as suspicious—secure pathogen storage, specialised containment facilities, international scientific cooperation and disease surveillance—are in fact standard functions of modern public-health laboratories. During the COVID-19 pandemic, the Lugar Center became one of Georgia’s key testing and diagnostic facilities, making its public-health role especially visible.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugar_Research_Center" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lugar Research Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lugar Research Center</span></span></span>

International observers have also been allowed to examine the facility. In 2018, experts and observers from multiple countries visited and reviewed the laboratory under mechanisms associated with the Biological Weapons Convention. Participants publicly reported that the centre operated transparently and found no evidence supporting the allegations then circulating.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://civil.ge/archives/266268" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: civil.ge">[Civil Georgia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">civil.ge</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">iaForeign Experts: Lugar Lab is transparent in its activities18 Nov 2018 — The review was carried out pursuant to the Biological Weapo...</span></span></span>

## How the allegations changed while the core story endured

One of the most revealing aspects of the campaign is that the accusations rarely stayed the same.

Early versions focused on the claim that the laboratory concealed an American biological-weapons programme near Russia’s borders. Later allegations asserted that scientists had conducted secret experiments on human beings. In 2018, former Georgian security official Igor Giorgadze, living in Russia, publicised documents that he claimed proved unethical testing and suspicious deaths. These allegations received extensive publicity but were challenged by journalists, scientists and international observers who found no evidence for the dramatic conclusions being promoted.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_war_during_the_Russo-Georgian_War" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Information war during the Russo-Georgian War</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Information war during the Russo-Georgian War</span></span></span>

Other claims followed:

## Why the claims sounded believable to some audiences

Disinformation campaigns rarely succeed by inventing something completely unfamiliar. They are more effective when they build upon existing fears.

Biological research naturally involves subjects that many people find alarming: deadly diseases, laboratory containment systems, specialised equipment and restricted access. Most citizens cannot personally inspect such facilities or independently verify technical claims. That information gap creates opportunities for speculation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gpwmdcounterdisinfo.com/policy-briefs/the-lugar-centre-disinformation-campaign/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gpwmdcounterdisinfo.com">[Countering WMD Disinformation]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gpwmdcounterdisinfo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Countering WMD DisinformationThe Lugar Centre disinformation campaignThe first highly visible, contemporary and multichannel disinformati...</span></span></span>

The Lugar Center also sits at the intersection of several emotionally charged subjects:<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugar_Research_Center" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lugar Research Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lugar Research Center</span></span></span>


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Public fears about epidemics and emerging diseases.
* Distrust of foreign governments.
* Suspicion of military research.
* Tensions between Russia and Georgia after the collapse of the Soviet Union and the 2008 war.
* The complexity of modern bioscience, which can be difficult for non-specialists to evaluate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://euvsdisinfo.eu/georgia/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: euvsdisinfo.eu">[euvsdisinfo.eu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">euvsdisinfo.eu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Georgia ArchivesDiscover EUvsDisinfo&#x27;s coverage on Georgia, exposing Russian disinformation campaigns targeting its independen...</span></span></span>

Because these themes already carried emotional weight, dramatic claims about secret experiments or hidden weapons programmes could spread even without strong evidence. The narrative did not require audiences to understand laboratory science; it only required them to suspect that something dangerous might be concealed behind technical expertise.

## How investigators and officials responded

The response to the allegations combined scientific transparency, diplomatic rebuttals and independent review.

Georgian authorities repeatedly invited foreign experts and observers to inspect the facility. American and Georgian officials publicly denied that biological-weapons work was taking place, emphasising that activities at the centre complied with international obligations under the Biological Weapons Convention. International specialists who examined the laboratory likewise reported no evidence supporting the sensational accusations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://civil.ge/archives/266268" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: civil.ge">[civil.ge]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">civil.ge</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">iaForeign Experts: Lugar Lab is transparent in its activities18 Nov 2018 — The review was carried out pursuant to the Biological Weapo...</span></span></span>

Fact-checking organisations, investigative journalists and scientific commentators also examined specific claims. The widely publicised allegation that dozens of people had died during secret drug experiments, for example, became a recurring focus of scrutiny. Investigators argued that the documents being cited did not support the conclusions being drawn from them and that the claims misrepresented ordinary medical records and research activity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://euvsdisinfo.eu/report/73-people-died-as-a-result-of-experiments-in-lugar-lab/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: euvsdisinfo.eu">[EUvsDisinfo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">euvsdisinfo.eu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on euvsdisinfo.eu.</span></span></span>

The laboratory’s supporters also pointed to a practical problem for the conspiracy theory: a secret weapons programme would be difficult to reconcile with years of international cooperation, visiting researchers, public-health work and external inspections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://civil.ge/archives/266268" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: civil.ge">[Civil Georgia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">civil.ge</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">iaForeign Experts: Lugar Lab is transparent in its activities18 Nov 2018 — The review was carried out pursuant to the Biological Weapo...</span></span></span>

## Why dangerous-pathogen research attracts conspiracy claims

The Lugar Center is not unique. Around the world, facilities that study dangerous pathogens often become targets of rumours and conspiratorial thinking.

Several factors make such institutions vulnerable:

**Invisible threats.** Unlike factories or military bases, laboratories work with microscopic organisms that cannot be directly observed by the public.

**Technical complexity.** Scientific procedures can be difficult to explain in simple terms, making misleading interpretations easier to circulate.

**Restricted access.** Security measures that exist for safety reasons can be reframed as evidence of secrecy.

**Historical precedents.** Genuine biological-weapons programmes existed in the twentieth century, allowing false accusations to borrow the language of real historical abuses.

**Political usefulness.** Claims about secret laboratories can be used to undermine trust in governments, international cooperation or foreign partners.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gpwmdcounterdisinfo.com/policy-briefs/the-lugar-centre-disinformation-campaign/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gpwmdcounterdisinfo.com">[gpwmdcounterdisinfo.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gpwmdcounterdisinfo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Countering WMD DisinformationThe Lugar Centre disinformation campaignThe first highly visible, contemporary and multichannel disinformati...</span></span></span>

Researchers examining biological-weapons disinformation have noted that allegations surrounding the Lugar Center later echoed in broader claims about laboratories elsewhere, including narratives promoted during the war in Ukraine.
