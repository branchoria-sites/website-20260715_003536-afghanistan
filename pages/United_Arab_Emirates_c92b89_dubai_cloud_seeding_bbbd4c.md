## Introduction

The dramatic floods that struck Dubai and other parts of the [United Arab Emirates]({{ 'united-arab-emirates/' | relative_url }}) in April 2024 quickly generated a global explanation: cloud seeding must have caused the disaster. Videos of submerged roads, flooded airport runways and stranded vehicles spread across social media alongside claims that the UAE had accidentally engineered its own weather catastrophe. The story sounded plausible because the country openly operates one of the world's best-known cloud-seeding programmes.

Yet the evidence points in a different direction. Meteorologists, climate researchers and the UAE's own weather authorities concluded that the flooding was caused by an unusually powerful regional storm system that affected a large area of the Arabian Peninsula. Cloud seeding became attached to the event largely because many people already knew the UAE used the technology, while dramatic images encouraged a simple explanation for a complex weather event. The episode became one of the most widely repeated weather myths associated with the modern UAE.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/what-caused-storm-that-brought-dubai-standstill-2024-04-17/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[reuters.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">What caused Dubai floods?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Experts cite climate change...April 17, 2024 — 18 Apr 2024 — What caused Dubai floods? ## Why the Claim Sounded Plausible

The cloud-seeding explanation did not emerge from nowhere. The UAE has invested in weather-modification research for years and regularly publicises efforts to increase rainfall in an arid environment. When news reports noted that cloud-seeding aircraft had flown missions in preceding days, many readers assumed a direct connection between those operations and the record-breaking rainfall.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/dubai-international-airport-says-it-is-experiencing-significant-disruption-due-2024-04-17/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[Reuters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Clean up begins after at least one dead in heavy UAE rain...April 17, 2024 — 17 Apr 2024 — Bloomberg earlier quoted the agency as...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 17, 2024</span></span></span>

Several factors helped the claim spread:

As a result, the idea spread internationally before detailed meteorological assessments had been widely circulated.

## What Cloud Seeding Can and Cannot Do

Understanding why experts rejected the claim requires understanding what cloud seeding actually does.

Cloud seeding is not a method for creating storms from clear skies. Aircraft or ground-based systems introduce particles into existing clouds in an attempt to encourage water droplets or ice crystals to form and grow. The technique is intended to increase precipitation from clouds that already contain moisture. Even supporters generally describe its effects as modest rather than transformative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/what-caused-storm-that-brought-dubai-standstill-2024-04-17/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[reuters.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">What caused Dubai floods?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Experts cite climate change...April 17, 2024 — 18 Apr 2024 — What caused Dubai floods? Meteorologists repeatedly emphasised that cloud seeding cannot manufacture a giant weather system. As Reuters reported, experts noted that the process cannot create clouds "from nothing" and first requires substantial atmospheric moisture already to be present.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/what-caused-storm-that-brought-dubai-standstill-2024-04-17/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[Reuters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">What caused Dubai floods?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Experts cite climate change...April 17, 2024 — 18 Apr 2024 — What caused Dubai floods? Scientists also pointed to a mismatch of scale. Individual cloud-seeding operations work on local cloud processes, whereas the April 2024 event involved a vast regional storm affecting multiple countries. A University of Reading meteorologist described a "fundamental mismatch of scale" between the seeding process and the enormous weather system responsible for the flooding.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reading.ac.uk/news/2024/Expert-Comment/Cloud-seeding-did-not-cause-Dubai-floods-expert-says" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reading.ac.uk">[University of Reading]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reading.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Cloud seeding did not cause Dubai floods expert says</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cloud seeding did not cause Dubai floods expert says</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because many online discussions treated cloud seeding as a form of weather control capable of generating storms at will. In reality, even researchers who support cloud-seeding programmes generally describe them as tools for marginally influencing precipitation under suitable conditions, not creating unprecedented regional deluges.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mei.edu/publication/debunking-role-cloud-seeding-april-arabian-gulf-floods/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mei.edu">[Middle East Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mei.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Middle East Institute Debunking the role of cloud seeding in the April Arabian</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Middle East InstituteDebunking the role of cloud seeding in the April Arabian...May 29, 2024 — Similarly, previous analysis reports an a...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 29, 2024</span></span></span>

## How Experts Traced the Wider Storm

Investigations into the flood focused not on local cloud-modification activities but on the large-scale atmospheric conditions present across the region.

Researchers from World Weather Attribution found that the event was associated with a powerful low-pressure system that developed from weather patterns extending far beyond the UAE. The same broader system contributed to severe rainfall across Oman and other parts of the region.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.worldweatherattribution.org/heavy-precipitation-hitting-vulnerable-communities-in-the-uae-and-oman-becoming-an-increasing-threat-as-the-climate-warms/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: worldweatherattribution.org">[worldweatherattribution.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">worldweatherattribution.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Heavy precipitation hitting vulnerable communities in the...25 Apr 2024 — From April 14 to15 2024, the United Arab Emirates (UAE) and th...</span></span></span>

The geographical footprint alone posed a problem for the cloud-seeding theory. Heavy rain and flooding were not confined to Dubai. Oman suffered deadly flooding, and severe weather affected multiple Gulf states. The scale of the event was consistent with a regional meteorological disturbance rather than a local intervention.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.worldweatherattribution.org/heavy-precipitation-hitting-vulnerable-communities-in-the-uae-and-oman-becoming-an-increasing-threat-as-the-climate-warms/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: worldweatherattribution.org">[worldweatherattribution.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">worldweatherattribution.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Heavy precipitation hitting vulnerable communities in the...25 Apr 2024 — From April 14 to15 2024, the United Arab Emirates (UAE) and th...</span></span></span>

## How a Weather Myth Took Hold

The cloud-seeding story is notable because it was not a classic hoax with a single inventor. Instead, it was a modern information cascade: a real technology became attached to a real disaster through misunderstanding, selective reporting and online amplification.
