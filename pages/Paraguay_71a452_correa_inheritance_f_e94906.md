## Introduction

The so-called Correa fortune is one of South America's longest-running inheritance legends and one of [Paraguay]({{ 'how-paraguays-most-persistent-deceptions/' | relative_url }})'s most persistent fraud stories. Unlike many hoaxes built on completely invented foundations, this one grew from a real nineteenth-century landowner, a genuine estate dispute and decades of confusing legal claims. That combination gave the story unusual staying power. People were not being asked to believe in a fictional treasure. They were being told that a vast inheritance already existed and that they merely needed the correct documents to claim their share.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2024/08/07/oro-caido-del-cielo-y-herencia-del-comendador-correa-una-fantastica-extendida-y-repetida-estafa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.com.py">[ABC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ABC“Oro caído del cielo” y la herencia del comendador Correa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 8, 2024 — 7 Aug 2024 — Esta vez, hay más de 300 personas estafadas con las gestiones realizadas para acceder a parte del patrimoni...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 8, 2024</span></span></span>

In Paraguay, the legend repeatedly resurfaced as fraudsters sold supposed inheritance rights, family links and legal paperwork connected to the estate of Brazilian businessman and landowner Domingos Faustino Correa, who died in 1873. The promised reward was enormous, often described as worth billions of dollars, while the obstacles to payment were explained away as the normal complications of a century-old legal battle. By 2024 Paraguayan prosecutors alleged that hundreds of victims had purchased forged or invalid rights to the supposed fortune.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2024/08/07/mas-de-300-estafados-bajo-promesa-de-recibir-herencia-del-comendador-correa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hoy.com.py">[Hoy Paraguay]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hoy.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Hoy Paraguay Más de 300 estafados bajo promesa de recibir herencia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Hoy ParaguayMás de 300 estafados bajo promesa de recibir herencia...August 7, 2024 — 7 Aug 2024 — La Fiscalía logró desbaratar una organ...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 7, 2024</span></span></span>

## The Real Estate Dispute Behind the Legend

The reason the Correa story proved so durable is that there was a real Domingos Faustino Correa. Historical records place him in nineteenth-century Brazil, where he accumulated extensive property and became a prominent figure. After his death, disputes surrounding his estate and inheritance generated a long and complicated legal history.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domingos_Faustino_Correia" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Domingos Faustino Correia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Domingos Faustino Correia</span></span></span>

Over time, stories about the estate grew far beyond what can be firmly documented. Claims circulated that Correa had left enormous tracts of land, mineral wealth and other assets spread across South America. Some versions asserted that governments had improperly absorbed portions of the inheritance and that rightful heirs would eventually recover the fortune after generations of litigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domingos_Faustino_Correia" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Domingos Faustino Correia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Domingos Faustino Correia</span></span></span>

This mixture of fact and speculation created ideal conditions for deception. The estate was real. Historical paperwork existed. Legal proceedings had indeed lasted for decades. Those truths made it much easier for later promoters to add unsupported claims about unimaginable wealth, hidden assets and imminent payouts.

## How Forged Rights Were Sold to Supposed Heirs

The fraud mechanism was remarkably simple.

Rather than claiming ownership of the fortune themselves, promoters sold access to it. Potential victims were told that they might be descendants of relatives entitled to inherit from Correa's estate. To secure their future share, they needed to purchase assignments of inheritance rights, legal representation, genealogical research or administrative processing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2024/08/07/mas-de-300-estafados-bajo-promesa-de-recibir-herencia-del-comendador-correa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hoy.com.py">[Hoy Paraguay]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hoy.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Hoy Paraguay Más de 300 estafados bajo promesa de recibir herencia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Hoy ParaguayMás de 300 estafados bajo promesa de recibir herencia...August 7, 2024 — 7 Aug 2024 — La Fiscalía logró desbaratar una organ...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 7, 2024</span></span></span>

The documents often appeared official. They used legal terminology, references to succession proceedings and detailed family histories. To many buyers, the paperwork looked far more convincing than the crude promises associated with conventional advance-fee scams.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2024/08/07/mas-de-300-estafados-bajo-promesa-de-recibir-herencia-del-comendador-correa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hoy.com.py">[Hoy Paraguay]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hoy.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Hoy Paraguay Más de 300 estafados bajo promesa de recibir herencia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Hoy ParaguayMás de 300 estafados bajo promesa de recibir herencia...August 7, 2024 — 7 Aug 2024 — La Fiscalía logró desbaratar una organ...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 7, 2024</span></span></span>

The scheme exploited several psychological advantages:

* **A genuine historical figure** gave the story credibility.
* **Legal complexity** made verification difficult.
* **Huge promised returns** encouraged risk-taking.
* **Official-looking documents** created an impression of legitimacy.
* **Cross-border family networks** allowed the story to spread among communities in several countries.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2024/08/07/oro-caido-del-cielo-y-herencia-del-comendador-correa-una-fantastica-extendida-y-repetida-estafa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.com.py">[ABC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ABC“Oro caído del cielo” y la herencia del comendador Correa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 8, 2024 — 7 Aug 2024 — Esta vez, hay más de 300 personas estafadas con las gestiones realizadas para acceder a parte del patrimoni...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 8, 2024</span></span></span>

The inheritance legend circulated not only in Paraguay but also in Brazil, Argentina, Uruguay and Chile, constantly finding new groups of supposed heirs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2024/08/07/oro-caido-del-cielo-y-herencia-del-comendador-correa-una-fantastica-extendida-y-repetida-estafa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.com.py">[ABC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ABC“Oro caído del cielo” y la herencia del comendador Correa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 8, 2024 — 7 Aug 2024 — Esta vez, hay más de 300 personas estafadas con las gestiones realizadas para acceder a parte del patrimoni...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 8, 2024</span></span></span>

## Why Delays and Sunk Costs Sustained Belief

Most frauds collapse when promised results fail to appear. The Correa story survived because delay was built into the claim itself.

Participants were told they were dealing with a nineteenth-century estate, international property disputes and generations of unresolved litigation. Long waits therefore appeared normal rather than suspicious. Every missed deadline could be explained as another court proceeding, another appeal or another administrative obstacle.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2024/08/07/oro-caido-del-cielo-y-herencia-del-comendador-correa-una-fantastica-extendida-y-repetida-estafa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.com.py">[ABC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ABC“Oro caído del cielo” y la herencia del comendador Correa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 8, 2024 — 7 Aug 2024 — Esta vez, hay más de 300 personas estafadas con las gestiones realizadas para acceder a parte del patrimoni...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 8, 2024</span></span></span>

The structure also exploited the sunk-cost effect. Once people had spent money on documents, membership fees or legal services, abandoning the claim meant accepting that the investment was lost. Continuing to believe often felt emotionally easier than admitting the possibility of deception.

Many versions of the story promised extraordinary rewards. Local reporting on the modern scam noted claims that individuals could eventually receive millions of dollars from the inheritance. Such figures encouraged victims to view additional payments as small sacrifices compared with the life-changing fortune supposedly awaiting them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.facebook.com/Unicanal/videos/%EF%B8%8F-la-herencia-del-comendador-sistema-de-estafa-de-los-90-dej%C3%B3-m%C3%A1s-de-300-v%C3%ADctima/1015547140064719/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facebook.com">[Facebook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Facebook▪️ La herencia del comendador Sistema de estafa de los 90...La herencia del comendador Sistema de estafa de los 90 dejó más de 3...</span></span></span>

Because the alleged payout remained permanently in the future, the scheme could continue even when no inheritance money ever arrived.

## The 2024 Paraguayan Investigation

In August 2024, Paraguay's Public Prosecutor's Office announced action against individuals suspected of selling fraudulent assignments of inheritance rights linked to the Correa estate. Investigators alleged that forged documents had been marketed to large numbers of people seeking access to the supposed fortune. Reports stated that more than 300 victims had been identified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2024/08/07/mas-de-300-estafados-bajo-promesa-de-recibir-herencia-del-comendador-correa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hoy.com.py">[Hoy Paraguay]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hoy.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Hoy Paraguay Más de 300 estafados bajo promesa de recibir herencia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Hoy ParaguayMás de 300 estafados bajo promesa de recibir herencia...August 7, 2024 — 7 Aug 2024 — La Fiscalía logró desbaratar una organ...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 7, 2024</span></span></span>

Authorities seized documents and electronic equipment while examining financial transactions connected with the operation. ## Why the Correa Fortune Never Arrived

The central promise of the Correa inheritance story is that an enormous fortune remains available and that heirs are perpetually on the verge of receiving it. Yet generation after generation has heard the same promise without the promised distribution ever occurring.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2024/08/07/oro-caido-del-cielo-y-herencia-del-comendador-correa-una-fantastica-extendida-y-repetida-estafa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.com.py">[ABC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ABC“Oro caído del cielo” y la herencia del comendador Correa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 8, 2024 — 7 Aug 2024 — Esta vez, hay más de 300 personas estafadas con las gestiones realizadas para acceder a parte del patrimoni...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 8, 2024</span></span></span>

The enduring appeal comes from three elements working together:

First, the story contains a real historical core. Correa existed, and disputes over his estate genuinely occurred.

Second, the legal history is sufficiently complicated that most people cannot easily verify every claim. Complexity becomes a substitute for evidence.

Third, believers are encouraged to focus on future possibilities rather than past failures. Each delay becomes proof that the process is still underway rather than evidence that the promised payout may never materialise.

For students of hoaxes and frauds in Paraguay, the Correa affair is a powerful example of how authenticity can become a tool of deception. The fraud did not succeed because people believed in something entirely imaginary. It succeeded because a real man, a real estate dispute and real historical records provided just enough truth to support an endless promise of wealth that always seemed close, yet never arrived.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2024/08/07/oro-caido-del-cielo-y-herencia-del-comendador-correa-una-fantastica-extendida-y-repetida-estafa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.com.py">[com.py]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ABC“Oro caído del cielo” y la herencia del comendador Correa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 8, 2024 — 7 Aug 2024 — Esta vez, hay más de 300 personas estafadas con las gestiones realizadas para acceder a parte del patrimoni...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 8, 2024</span></span></span>
