## Introduction

No, India did not buy 28 Maldivian islands. The claim was a piece of political misinformation that spread online in August 2024 after genuine footage from an official [Maldives]({{ 'which-maldives-stories-were-hoaxes-or/' | relative_url }})–India ceremony was given a completely false explanation. Viral posts alleged that President Mohamed Muizzu had signed an agreement transferring 28 islands to India, often presenting the supposed deal as proof of either Indian influence or Maldivian surrender. In reality, the event concerned the inauguration and handover of water and sanitation infrastructure projects built on 28 Maldivian islands with Indian financial assistance. Fact-checkers, government agencies and official statements all confirmed that no territorial transfer had taken place.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.boomlive.in/fact-check/fake-news-india-buys-28-islands-from-maldives-president-mohamed-muizzu-fact-check-26191" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: boomlive.in">[boomlive.in]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">boomlive.in</span><span class="citation-popover-title">BOOMNo, Maldives Did Not Hand Over 28 Islands To India</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 14, 2024 — 14 Aug 2024 — FACT CHECKSocial media users misinterpreted a &#x27;handover ceremony&#x27; of water and sewerage facilities, which...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 14, 2024</span></span></span>

The episode is a useful example of how modern misinformation often works. Rather than inventing photographs or forging documents, it takes authentic images and videos and supplies them with a misleading story. A routine development ceremony was transformed online into an alleged sovereignty crisis.

## What the Viral Posts Alleged

The rumour spread through social media posts, videos and graphics claiming that the Maldives had “handed over” or “sold” 28 islands to India. Some versions asserted that President Muizzu had personally signed an agreement transferring territory, while others portrayed the supposed transaction as a major geopolitical victory for India.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thequint.com/news/webqoof/false-claim-india-bought-28-maldives-islands-fact-check" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thequint.com">[thequint.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thequint.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">False Claims About India &#x27;Buying&#x27; 28 Islands...August 21, 2024 — 21 Aug 2024 — A video is being shared with the claim with image...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 21, 2024</span></span></span>

The claim gained attention because it appeared during a period of heightened discussion about relations between the two countries. Earlier disputes over tourism, diplomatic remarks and the presence of Indian personnel in the Maldives had already made questions of sovereignty and foreign influence emotionally charged subjects. In such an atmosphere, a dramatic territorial-transfer story seemed plausible to some readers even though no supporting evidence was provided.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.telugupost.com/english-factcheck/maldives-did-not-handover-28-islands-to-india-1547954" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: telugupost.com">[telugupost.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">telugupost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Telgu Post Fact Check: Maldives did not handover 28 islands to India</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Telgu PostFact Check: Maldives did not handover 28 islands to IndiaAugust 14, 2024 — 14 Aug 2024 — In January 2024, relations between Ind...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 14, 2024</span></span></span>

## What the Handover Ceremony Actually Covered

The event at the centre of the rumour was linked to water supply and sewerage projects completed on 28 inhabited islands of the Maldives. The projects were financed through Indian assistance, including credit facilities supported by the Export-Import Bank of India. During Indian External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar's visit to the Maldives in August 2024, the completed facilities were formally inaugurated and handed over for use.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.boomlive.in/fact-check/fake-news-india-buys-28-islands-from-maldives-president-mohamed-muizzu-fact-check-26191" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: boomlive.in">[boomlive.in]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">boomlive.in</span><span class="citation-popover-title">BOOMNo, Maldives Did Not Hand Over 28 Islands To India</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 14, 2024 — 14 Aug 2024 — FACT CHECKSocial media users misinterpreted a &#x27;handover ceremony&#x27; of water and sewerage facilities, which...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 14, 2024</span></span></span>

Official statements from both governments described the ceremony as part of development cooperation. President Muizzu publicly referred to the handover of water and sewerage projects and thanked India for its support. Nothing in the official announcements suggested any transfer of territory, ownership of islands or sovereignty rights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://factly.in/the-claim-that-india-purchased-28-islands-from-the-maldives-is-fake/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: factly.in">[FACTLY]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">factly.in</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the claim that india purchased 28 islands from the maldives is fake</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The claim that India purchased 28 islands from...14 Aug 2024 — There have been no reports of the central government under Prime Mi...</span></span></span>

## How Genuine Footage Gained a False Meaning

This case illustrates a common misinformation technique: recaptioning. The photographs and videos were authentic, but the explanation attached to them was not. Because viewers could see a real ceremony involving senior officials, the images carried an air of credibility. The falsehood entered through the accompanying text rather than through visual manipulation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.boomlive.in/fact-check/fake-news-india-buys-28-islands-from-maldives-president-mohamed-muizzu-fact-check-26191" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: boomlive.in">[BOOM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">boomlive.in</span><span class="citation-popover-title">BOOMNo, Maldives Did Not Hand Over 28 Islands To India</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 14, 2024 — 14 Aug 2024 — FACT CHECKSocial media users misinterpreted a &#x27;handover ceremony&#x27; of water and sewerage facilities, which...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 14, 2024</span></span></span>

Several fact-checking organisations traced the rumour to social media posts that incorrectly described the event as a territorial agreement. The visual evidence itself did not show islands being sold, transferred or leased. Instead, viewers were encouraged to accept a political interpretation unsupported by any treaty, official document or government announcement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.boomlive.in/fact-check/fake-news-india-buys-28-islands-from-maldives-president-mohamed-muizzu-fact-check-26191" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: boomlive.in">[boomlive.in]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">boomlive.in</span><span class="citation-popover-title">BOOMNo, Maldives Did Not Hand Over 28 Islands To India</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 14, 2024 — 14 Aug 2024 — FACT CHECKSocial media users misinterpreted a &#x27;handover ceremony&#x27; of water and sewerage facilities, which...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 14, 2024</span></span></span>

The story spread quickly because it connected to existing narratives. Supporters and critics of closer India–Maldives relations could both fit the claim into broader arguments about influence in the Indian Ocean. A dramatic allegation about national territory naturally attracted more attention than a technical announcement about sanitation infrastructure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.telugupost.com/english-factcheck/maldives-did-not-handover-28-islands-to-india-1547954" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: telugupost.com">[Telgu Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">telugupost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Telgu Post Fact Check: Maldives did not handover 28 islands to India</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Telgu PostFact Check: Maldives did not handover 28 islands to IndiaAugust 14, 2024 — 14 Aug 2024 — In January 2024, relations between Ind...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 14, 2024</span></span></span>

## How the Claim Was Debunked

The rumour was publicly challenged by multiple fact-checking organisations and by official government channels. India's Press Information Bureau fact-check service labelled the allegation fake and clarified that the ceremony concerned development projects rather than any transfer of islands.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://x.com/PIBFactCheck/status/1823380364313887061?lang=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: x.com">[X (formerly Twitter)]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">x.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">X (formerly Twitter)PIB Fact CheckSeveral social media posts claim that the President of Maldives has signed an agreement to hand over 28...</span></span></span>

Independent fact-checkers reached the same conclusion after examining the original footage, official press releases and statements from the participants. They found no evidence of any agreement involving the sale, lease or transfer of Maldivian territory.
