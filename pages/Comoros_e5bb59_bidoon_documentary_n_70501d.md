## Introduction

The [Comoros]({{ 'when-official-stories-in-comoros-fell/' | relative_url }}) passport affair became controversial not simply because passports were issued, but because of what those passports were meant to achieve. For thousands of stateless Gulf residents known as the Bidoon, Comorian nationality was presented as a practical solution to a long-running legal problem. On paper, a person who lacked any nationality could become a citizen of Comoros and receive a passport. In reality, many recipients had never lived in Comoros, had no social ties to the islands, and were not expected to relocate there. Critics argued that the arrangement transformed stateless people into foreign nationals on paper while leaving many of the underlying problems of exclusion, insecurity and limited rights unresolved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com/index.php/journal/article/download/639/391" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com">[statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Statelessness &amp; Citizenship ReviewProhibition of Deportation for Bidoons with Comoros PassportsAugust 21, 2024 — by HU Marambio · 2024 ·...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 21, 2024</span></span></span>

## Can a Passport Solve Statelessness Without Citizenship?

The central idea behind the scheme was deceptively simple. If a stateless person could be assigned a recognised nationality, governments would no longer have to classify that individual as stateless. Comoros, through its economic citizenship programme, offered a nationality that could be granted to people who had no meaningful connection to the country. Gulf governments and officials promoting the arrangement argued that this would provide documentation, legal identity and a route out of statelessness.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://odi.org/en/insights/cash-for-citizenship-rich-arab-countries-may-pay-poor-islands-to-take-stateless-bidoons/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: odi.org">[odi.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">odi.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Think changeCash for citizenship: rich Arab countries may pay poor...15 Nov 2016 — A deal that would grant Comorian citizenship to...</span></span></span>

For critics, however, the distinction between legal status and lived reality was crucial. A passport can prove that a state recognises someone as a citizen, but citizenship is also a bundle of practical rights, political membership and social belonging. Many Bidoon who received or were offered Comorian nationality continued to live in the Gulf rather than in Comoros. Their day-to-day position depended far more on Gulf residency rules than on their formal connection to the Indian Ocean archipelago.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://globalcit.eu/comoran-citizenship-for-bidoons/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: globalcit.eu">[globalcit.eu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">globalcit.eu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Comoran citizenship for bidoonsJanuary 11, 2018 — 11 Jan 2018 — The forcing of the bidoons to accept Comoran passports has raise...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 11, 2018</span></span></span>

This created what scholars and campaigners often describe as a form of “documentary nationality”: a citizenship that exists primarily in administrative records and travel documents rather than through participation in a national community.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://globalcit.eu/the-problem-with-the-comoros-solution-affect-citizenship-statelessness-and-the-kuwaiti-bidoon/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: globalcit.eu">[globalcit.eu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">globalcit.eu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">affect, citizenship, statelessness and the Kuwaiti BidoonThe Kuwaiti bidoon are a subset of the nomadic population of Kuwait who...</span></span></span>

## Why Gulf Governments Wanted a Foreign Nationality

The Bidoon issue has challenged Gulf governments for decades. Large numbers of people in Kuwait and smaller numbers elsewhere in the region have long claimed entitlement to local citizenship but remain classified as non-citizens or stateless residents. Their status has attracted criticism from international organisations and human-rights groups.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedoon" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

Against that background, Comorian nationality offered several attractions to policymakers:<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comorian_nationality_law" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Comorian nationality law</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Comorian nationality law</span></span></span>

* **Administrative clarity:** A person recorded as a Comorian citizen could be classified as a foreign resident rather than a stateless individual.
* **International optics:** Governments facing criticism over stateless populations could argue that affected individuals now possessed a recognised nationality.
* **Migration control:** Authorities could regulate residency through ordinary immigration systems rather than through special arrangements for stateless people.
* **Political compromise:** The scheme appeared to avoid granting large numbers of people full citizenship in wealthier Gulf states.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://opiniojuris.org/2014/11/13/kuwait-bulk-orders-comoros-citizenship-stateless-bidoons/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: opiniojuris.org">[opiniojuris.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">opiniojuris.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">kuwait bulk orders comoros citizenship stateless bidoons</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Opinio JurisKuwait Bulk-Orders Comoros Citizenship for Stateless...13 Nov 2014 — Kuwait has long been criticized for its refusal to exte...</span></span></span>

The proposal became particularly associated with Kuwait, where discussions about granting Comorian nationality to large numbers of Bidoon drew international attention in the mid-2010s. Although the most ambitious plans encountered political resistance and were never fully realised in their original form, the broader idea influenced debates across the Gulf.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://opiniojuris.org/2014/11/13/kuwait-bulk-orders-comoros-citizenship-stateless-bidoons/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: opiniojuris.org">[Opinio Juris]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">opiniojuris.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">kuwait bulk orders comoros citizenship stateless bidoons</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Opinio JurisKuwait Bulk-Orders Comoros Citizenship for Stateless...13 Nov 2014 — Kuwait has long been criticized for its refusal to exte...</span></span></span>

In the United Arab Emirates, Comorian passports were reportedly issued to some stateless residents through arrangements connected to the economic citizenship programme. Human-rights observers later questioned whether possession of these passports genuinely improved recipients' legal security.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com/index.php/journal/article/download/639/391" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com">[statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Statelessness &amp; Citizenship ReviewProhibition of Deportation for Bidoons with Comoros PassportsAugust 21, 2024 — by HU Marambio · 2024 ·...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 21, 2024</span></span></span>

## What Documentary Citizenship Changed

The programme did produce real changes. It would be misleading to describe every Comorian passport issued under the scheme as fake. Many were genuine state documents issued through recognised channels. The controversy centred on what those documents actually accomplished.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comorian_passport" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Comorian passport</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Comorian passport</span></span></span>

* A formal nationality where none had existed before.
* A clearer legal category in dealings with government agencies.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com/index.php/journal/article/download/639/391" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com">[Statelessness &amp; Citizenship Review]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Statelessness &amp; Citizenship ReviewProhibition of Deportation for Bidoons with Comoros PassportsAugust 21, 2024 — by HU Marambio · 2024 ·...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 21, 2024</span></span></span>

Supporters argued that these were meaningful improvements. Statelessness can make everyday activities—from obtaining official papers to travelling internationally—extremely difficult. From that perspective, any recognised nationality represented progress.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com/index.php/journal/article/download/639/391" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com">[Statelessness &amp; Citizenship Review]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">statelessnessandcitizenshipreview.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Statelessness &amp; Citizenship ReviewProhibition of Deportation for Bidoons with Comoros PassportsAugust 21, 2024 — by HU Marambio · 2024 ·...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 21, 2024</span></span></span>

## Why Critics Said the Plan Moved the Problem

The strongest criticism was that the scheme addressed paperwork rather than belonging.

Human-rights advocates, legal scholars and citizenship researchers argued that the arrangement allowed states to redefine stateless people as foreign nationals without resolving disputes about their connection to the countries where they had been born and lived for generations. In this view, the problem was not the absence of a passport alone but the absence of secure membership in the society where people actually resided.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.statelessness.eu/updates/blog/investor-citizenship-must-not-be-used-remedy-statelessness" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: statelessness.eu">[statelessness.eu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">statelessness.eu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">European Network on StatelessnessInvestor citizenship must not be used as a remedy for...13 Jun 2019 — Once they had obtained the Comori...</span></span></span>

A recurring concern was that a Comorian passport could weaken claims to local citizenship.
